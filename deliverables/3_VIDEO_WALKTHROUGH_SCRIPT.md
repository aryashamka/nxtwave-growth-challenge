# 3-Minute Video Presentation Script
## NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge — Round 1 Walkthrough
**Target Duration:** Exactly 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Setup:** Screen sharing the Streamlit Dashboard + Webcam in top-right corner.

---

### [0:00 – 0:35] Part 1: The Core Problem & Strategic Insight
*(Look directly into the camera. Confident, energetic tone.)*

> "Hi NxtWave team! When approaching the challenge to get 500 final-year engineering students to register for the 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes' workshop with just ₹2,000, I started with the numbers.
> 
> Most applicants would spend that ₹2,000 on Instagram ads or build a basic landing page. But at standard student CPLs of ₹25 to ₹40, paid ads run dry after only 60 leads. And on mobile, landing page forms see a 60% drop-off.
> 
> So I asked: *Who is this student, and what actually moves them?*
> Final-year students are stressed about placements. Recruiters are throwing out generic CRUD resumes. They don't want another certificate of participation; they want **placement currency**.
> 
> Our hook is: **'Build & Deploy a Live AI Agent + Get 3 Interview-Ready Resume Bullet Points in 60 Minutes.'**"

---

### [0:35 – 1:30] Part 2: The Working Asset — Telegram Bot + Viral Growth Engine
*(Switch screen share to the Streamlit Dashboard and click into 'Interactive Bot Simulator'.)*

> "Instead of a static landing page, I built a working growth engine: an automated **Telegram Bot with built-in Viral Mechanics**, backed by a live **Streamlit Command Center**.
> 
> Let me show you how it works:
> 
> 1. **Zero-Friction 1-Tap Registration:** When a student opens the bot, Telegram already knows their identity. With two quick button clicks — selecting their College and Engineering Branch — they are instantly registered. No tedious 6-field forms.
> 2. **Instant Admit Pass & Unique Referral Link:** The bot generates their customized Workshop Pass and a personalized referral code, complete with pre-drafted WhatsApp text to share with friends.
> 3. **The Gamified Reward Ladder:** Students unlock rewards as they refer:
>    * 2 referrals: ATS-optimized AI Resume Bullet Points.
>    * 5 referrals: Full GitHub Starter Repository & 50 Interview Questions.
>    * 10 referrals: VIP Workshop Pass with Live Code Review.
> 4. **AI Project Ideator (`/ideas`):** Students can type `/ideas` right now to get an AI-tailored project blueprint and resume points matching their exact branch — CSE, ECE, or Mechanical.
> 5. **College vs College Pride Loop (`/leaderboard`):** Engineering students are fiercely loyal to their colleges. When the bot shows JNTU trailing Anna University by 10 registrations, students take ownership and blast the link to their batch groups."

---

### [1:30 – 2:20] Part 3: The Data & The Math to 500
*(Switch screen share to 'Executive Growth Dashboard' showing the KPI cards and 7-day velocity chart.)*

> "Now, here is the executive analytics dashboard that proves how the numbers work:
> 
> * **The ₹2,000 Budget Deployment:** We spend ₹1,200 as ₹100 performance micro-bounties for 12 Class Representatives who drive 25+ batchmates. That gives us our initial base of 275 verified leads.
> * **The Viral K-Factor:** Notice our viral coefficient here is **0.35**. That means every 3 direct registrants bring in 1 peer through the reward ladder, generating another 145 registrations at ₹0 acquisition cost.
> * Adding 80 registrations from targeted placement community drops gives us **500+ registrations at an effective blended CPA of just ₹3.42 per student**.
> * And crucially, the bot automates T-24h and T-1h reminder push notifications to solve the show-up problem."

---

### [2:20 – 3:00] Part 4: Critical Judgment & Reflection
*(Camera back in focus, wrap up with conviction.)*

> "Finally, how did I use AI in this process?
> AI originally suggested running Meta ads and building a 10-section syllabus page. I deliberately rejected both. Ads would have blown the budget, and long syllabi cause mobile drop-offs.
> 
> If I had another 24 hours, I would connect the WhatsApp Cloud API to mirror this flow natively on WhatsApp and build an automated post-workshop GitHub repo evaluator for instant LinkedIn verification.
> 
> I built this because growth isn't about guessing; it's about building scalable distribution engines, understanding user psychology, and shipping real assets with high bias to action.
> 
> Thank you, and I look forward to speaking with the NxtWave Growth team in the final interview!"

---

### Recording Tips for the Candidate:
1. **Pacing:** Speak with energy and clarity. 130–150 words per minute is ideal.
2. **Visuals:** Keep the live Streamlit dashboard open at `localhost:8501`. Trigger balloons on registration during the demo!
3. **Framing:** Ensure good lighting on your face and clean audio.
