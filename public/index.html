Decision 1: Budget Strategy

Paid Ads vs. Campus Micro-Bounties

What I Asked:

"How should I spend ₹2,000 to get 500 student registrations?"

What AI Suggested:

"Run Meta lead gen ads targeting engineering colleges."

What I Deliberately Rejected & Why:

Rejected paid ads. CPL is ₹25–40, giving only 50–70 leads. Pivoted 100% of budget into CR bounties and referral incentives, dropping CPA to ₹2.74.

Decision 2: Working Asset

10-Section Landing Page vs. 1-Tap Bot Flow

What I Asked:

"Design the registration landing page for the workshop."

What AI Suggested:

"Build a long React landing page with 6 form fields."

What I Deliberately Rejected & Why:

Rejected standard landing page. High mobile form friction leads to 60%+ drop-off. Replaced with 1-tap bot with viral loops and reminders.

Decision 3: Messaging Hook

Free Certificates vs. Placement Currency

What I Asked:

"What marketing hook converts engineering students?"

What AI Suggested:

"Offer a free Certificate of Participation."

What I Deliberately Rejected & Why:

Rejected participation certificates. Recruiters ignore them. Positioned around practical placement currency: ready-to-use resume bullet points.
